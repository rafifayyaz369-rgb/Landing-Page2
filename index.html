Learning how things work, then building on it.

 
   

My path has crossed offices, digital marketplaces, the road, and online products. Each environment worked by different rules, and each one taught me how a system really runs: who it serves, what keeps it moving, and where it breaks.

   

I treat every role as a place to learn. Administration gave me discipline and process. Trading gave me a feel for markets and trust. Driving gave me patience with people. Selling digital products tied it together into a view of how a small business operates end to end.

   

I am now focused on turning that range into something I build on purpose, with technology and entrepreneurship at the center.
